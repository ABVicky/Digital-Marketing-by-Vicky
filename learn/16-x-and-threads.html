🐦 Module 16: X (Twitter) & Threads

🟠 Stage 3 · Social, Email & Ads · ⏱️ ~2.5 hours

A courier strike begins on a Tuesday morning. Customers are already posting "Where is my order?" Mitti's competitors say nothing until Thursday. Meera posts at 10:40 AM: what is affected, what isn't, what customers can do, and when the next update will come. It's the most useful thing the studio ever publishes on X, and it contains no marketing at all.

X and Threads are conversation networks. For many businesses they're listening and response tools first. This module is short on purpose: learn enough to decide well.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • Decide whether to be active, listen only, or skip.
  • Write clear short posts and threads.
  • Use search for research and service.
  • Handle pile-ons, misinformation and crises.

1. Should You Be Here?

ChooseIf
Activeyour buyers, press, developers, finance, politics, sport or fan community talks here daily, and someone can reply fast
Listen and servecustomers mention or complain about you here
Reserve and skipbuyers aren't here and time is limited

Mitti's choice: reserve the name, pin contact details, listen weekly, post real updates. That's a complete strategy.

👔 Choosing not to use a platform is a decision, not a failure. Write the reason and a date to review it.

2. Profile

Name: Mitti Studio
Bio: Handmade ceramics and beginner classes in Kolkata.
Support: {{support_link}}, 10 AM-6 PM, Tue-Sun.
Link: the most useful page
Pinned: what we make, how to get help

Verification marks have different meanings and can be paid. Don't treat a badge as proof of identity or expertise. Protect your account with strong login security.

3. Writing Short

A good short post does one thing.

TypeExample
Observation"Most broken parcels we see fail at the corner, not the middle."
Tip"Photograph the box before opening. Claims get easier."
Question"Potters: what do you do with failed glazes?"
Update"Kolkata orders: one day late. Everything else is normal."
Opinion"'Handmade' should say whose hands."
Proofshort drop-test clip

❌ Weak

Exciting things coming soon!! Stay tuned!! 🚀🚀 #startup #hustle #grind

✅ Strong

We tested 4 packing methods, 10 drops each.
Paper-only: 6 broken.
Bubble wrap: 2.
Corrugated sleeve + paper: 0.
We've switched. Slower to pack, worth it.

Publish numbers only from tests you really ran, and describe the method when asked.

4. Threads (the format)

1  Result and why it matters
2  Context in one post
3-6 One step or proof each
7  Limits: what we don't know
8  One resource or question

Every post in the thread should stand alone, because any of them can be shared.

5. What We Know About Ranking

X has published parts of its recommendation code, and it's updated often; some parts aren't public. Blog posts disagree about links, paid accounts and posting tricks. A sensible approach:

  1. Say something worth replying to.
  2. Reply to people who matter in your field.
  3. Test link and no-link posts in your own account.
  4. Judge by useful visits and conversations.

Threads (the Meta app) also favours conversation. Reuse the idea, but rewrite for the place and don't cross-post complaints about the other platform.

7. Customer Service

  1. Reply publicly with acknowledgement.
  2. Move order details to a private, official channel.
  3. Give owner and time.
  4. Close the loop.
Sorry your set arrived chipped, Asha. Please send the order number to
our support link in bio, not here, so your details stay private. I'm
Meera, and I'll reply before 6 PM today.

Watch for fake support accounts replying to your customers. Publish your only official channels.

8. Journalists and Experts

❌ Weak pitch

Please cover our amazing startup!

✅ Strong pitch

You asked about small exporters and packing costs. We tested 4 methods
over 40 drops and can share raw results and photos. Our limitation:
one box size, one city. Happy to answer by 5 PM.

9. Crisis Basics

TypeResponse

Don't delete criticism, mass-block ordinary customers or let an angry founder reply at midnight.

10. Ads and Brand Safety

Test only with a specific reason, such as an event, launch or community. Review placement controls, adjacency options and current policies. Compare with your main channel using cost per qualified outcome, not impressions.

🏋️ Exercises

Exercise 16.1: Choose

Decide active/listen/skip for: a neighbourhood salon, a developer tool, a stock-news app, a wedding photographer.

View Reasoning

Salon: usually skip or listen. Developer tool: likely active or listen. Stock-news app: likely active, with strict compliance. Wedding photographer: usually visual networks first, so reserve and listen. Customer evidence can change any of these.

Exercise 16.2: Incident update

Payments fail for 40 minutes. Write the first post.

View Sample

"Card payments on our site are failing since about 2:10 PM. UPI works. If you were charged and have no confirmation, don't pay again; send your order email to {{support}}. Next update at 3 PM." Publish only verified facts.

Exercise 16.3: Listen

Run five saved searches for a week. Bring back three customer phrases and one content idea.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Listening can be enough.
  • Short posts need one clear point.
  • Be fast, factual and calm in a problem.
  • Distrust algorithm certainty.

✅ Quick Quiz

  1. Is "listen and serve" a valid strategy?
    • A) Yes
    • B) No
  2. A paid badge proves:
    • A) Expertise
    • B) Nothing by itself about truth
    • C) Official support
  3. First step in a crisis?
    • A) Post an ad
    • B) Pause scheduled posts and verify
    • C) Delete comments
View Answers
  1. A.
  2. B.
  3. B. Automated cheerful posts can make things worse.
