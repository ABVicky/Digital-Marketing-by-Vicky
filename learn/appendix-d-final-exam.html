Practical Tasks

Score each task 0–20: evidence 5, reasoning 5, accuracy 5, usable output 5. Pass: 70/100.

  1. Research and position: turn five real interview notes into a job and positioning statement.
  2. Economics: model low/base/high contribution, target CPA and a loss limit.
  3. Campaign: create one page, three different creatives and a tracking plan.
  4. Diagnosis: Mitti spends ₹30,000; gets 2,000 clicks, 1,500 landing-page views, 45 carts and 18 purchases. Recommend the next checks.
  5. Report: explain an inconclusive test honestly in one page.
View Task 4 Guide

CPC ₹15; purchase rate is 0.9% of clicks or 1.2% of page views; CPA ₹1,667, above ₹810 break-even. Check the click-to-page loss, query/creative match, product-to-cart friction, checkout, tracking and source quality. Don't scale. Choose the next evidence-gathering step.
