🛡️ Privacy, Law & Ethics

Module 25 · By Vicky Prasad Mahato · Updated 2026-11-23

🟣 Stage 4 · Measure & Scale · ⏱️ ~4 hours

If a shopkeeper followed you home, guessed your worries and wrote them on your door, you wouldn't call it personalization. Digital tools make similar behaviour easy and invisible.

⚠️ This module is education, not legal advice. Laws depend on place, industry and facts. Review official sources and use a qualified adviser.

🔑 In short: Ethical digital marketing collects only needed data, asks for specific permission, tells the truth, makes leaving easy and prepares for mistakes. In India, check DPDP phases, consumer-protection and ASCI rules, and get legal advice for real decisions.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Apply data minimization and consent.
  • Check claims, endorsements and offers.
  • Spot dark patterns.
  • Respond safely to incidents and complaints.

2. Consent

Use specific choices, plain notice, no pre-ticked box and withdrawal as easy as giving permission. Keep evidence. Don't assume one consent covers every purpose or channel.

India's DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 with phased application. Consent-manager provisions were scheduled for November 2026, and most core duties for May 2027 after 18 months. Sources differ by about a day; check the official rules and any later amendment.

Other places may apply GDPR/UK GDPR, ePrivacy, state privacy, telemarketing or anti-spam laws, sometimes because of where recipients live. Indian commercial SMS and calls also have telecom rules. Get the facts before sending.

A form makeover

❌ Weak

Phone *  Email *  Date of birth *
[x] I agree to everything.
Submit

✅ Strong

Email for your size guide *
[ ] Email me useful pottery offers about twice a month.
Privacy notice: what we collect, why, how long, sharing and choices.
[Send the guide]

The guide can be sent without marketing consent. The optional box isn't pre-ticked, and the privacy notice should be understandable at the point of collection. Your notice and legal basis need professional review for your facts.

Withdrawal and rights

Create one internal request process: verify identity proportionately, find relevant data, pause affected uses, respond within the applicable time, record the decision and inform processors. Don't demand excessive identity documents for a simple unsubscribe.

DPDP transition check

India's official rules were notified with phases extending to 2027. Today, document readiness gaps and confirm effective dates, then make a dated plan for notices, choices, rights, security, vendors, retention, children and incidents. Later amendments or guidance may change it.

3. Cookies and Tags

A banner isn't protection if tags fire before a choice. Test first visit, accept, reject, change and withdrawal. Keep "reject" understandable. Server-side tracking doesn't remove consent duties.

4. Claims and Reviews

❌ Risky

India's #1 mug. Guaranteed never to break.

✅ Supportable

Broken on arrival? Send a photo within 48 hours and we replace it.

Keep dated evidence for important claims. Comparisons must be fair. Reviews need a genuine source; testimonials mustn't hide that the result is unusual. Health, finance, employment, education and children need extra care.

Test claims before ads

"Guaranteed marks"exceptional and likely riskydescribe syllabus and support
"No side effects"qualified proof/reviewavoid unsupported health claim

❌ Weak testimonial

Earned ₹5 lakh in a month! (paid actor)

✅ Stronger testimonial

"The worksheet showed me two product costs I'd forgotten."
— {{permissioned customer}}, {{date}}. Individual experience.

A disclosure can't make a false or unfair claim true.

5. Influencers and AI

Make material connections obvious at the start or where people see the endorsement. Don't hide disclosure after "more". Agree on claim approval and correction. Check ASCI and consumer-law rules in India and local rules elsewhere. Obtain permission for likeness and voice.

7. Responsible Targeting

Don't infer or exploit sensitive conditions. Avoid excluding people from important opportunities. Review outputs by group where lawful and appropriate. Give vulnerable people more protection, not more pressure.

8. Incident Plan

  1. Stop the harmful send/tag/access.
  2. Preserve logs without spreading personal data.
  3. Escalate to the named privacy/security owner.
  4. Assess who and what is affected.
  5. Meet required notice duties.
  6. Correct, document and prevent recurrence.

Prepare this before you need it. Don't quietly delete evidence.

👔 Newspaper test: would the customer think this practice was fair if you explained it plainly on the front page?

9. Platform Rules and Regulated Work

Platform approval doesn't prove legality, and legality doesn't prove a platform will approve. Keep current official policies for:

  • Health and medicines.
  • Finance, loans and investments.
  • Employment, housing and credit.
  • Education outcomes and minors.
  • Alcohol, gambling and other restricted products.
  • Political and social issues.
  • Copyright, trademark and endorsements.
  • Commercial messages, calls and email.

Have a qualified person review high-risk claims. Avoid private medical or financial attributes in audience choices. Don't upload customer data before confirming consent/legal basis, purpose, platform terms and deletion.

💻 URL Privacy Check

from urllib.parse import urlparse, parse_qs

private_keys = {"email", "phone", "name", "token"}

def risky_query_keys(url):
    keys = set(parse_qs(urlparse(url).query))
    return sorted(keys & private_keys)

print(risky_query_keys("https://example.com/thanks?utm_source=email&email=x@example.com"))

This catches only known names. Review forms, hashes, event payloads, logs, referrers and custom parameters too. Never use real customer data in this test.

11. Marketing to Children and Vulnerable People

Use age-appropriate language, stronger defaults, parental involvement where required, no exploitative pressure and limited data. Don't target children for tracking or collect extra data because it improves a segment. Stop using fear, shame or medical vulnerability to increase conversion.

Accessibility and dignity

Don't present disability as tragedy to sell a product. Ask people how they want to be described, pay contributors fairly and test the actual task. Inclusive images don't replace accessible checkout.

14. Incident Tabletop

Scenario: a staff member emails 300 workshop customers with everyone's addresses visible.

TimePractice action
0 minstop further sends, preserve message and recipient list
5 mintell named privacy/security owner
20 minassess data, number, recipients and continuing exposure
45 minget required legal/privacy advice, contain and notify as required
Latercorrect process, train, document decision

Don't promise a universal notification time here: laws and facts differ. The lesson is to have the contact, evidence and decision process ready.

🔑 The safest campaign is often the one with less data, a clearer choice and a promise the business can keep.

15. Ethics Scorecard

Score 0–2: truthful, necessary, expected, reversible, accessible, fair, secure, accountable. Anything scoring zero needs a named person, a fix and a review. A campaign with a high conversion rate doesn't average away a privacy failure.

🏋️ Exercises

Exercise 25.1

Review: "Only 2 left!" appears forever; newsletter is pre-ticked; cancellation is by phone only.

View Solution

Possible fake scarcity, invalid/unclear permission and a cancellation trap. Use true stock, clear choice and an easy online cancellation, then check local law.

Exercise 25.2

Make a data map for one form. Delete unnecessary fields.

Exercise 25.3: Claim check

Rewrite: "Guaranteed weight loss in 7 days, approved by doctors."

View Solution

Remove it until qualified, lawful evidence and medical review support a specific claim. Describe the product factually, avoid implying guaranteed outcomes or a universal approval, and follow platform and health rules. Don't try to hide the same claim in a testimonial.

Exercise 25.4: Incident practice

Write a one-page incident contact and first-hour checklist for your business, using no real customer data.

View Success Check

Names role-based contacts, containment, evidence, legal review, affected-data assessment, communication owner and later process repair. It doesn't assume one notification rule for every place.

❓ Common Questions

What is DPDP and how does it affect marketing?

DPDP is India's personal-data protection law. The 2025 rules apply in phases, with most core duties scheduled for May 2027. Marketers should prepare clear notices, specific consent, easy withdrawal, retention limits and vendor controls.

What are dark patterns in marketing?

They are designs that trick or pressure people, such as fake scarcity, hidden fees, pre-ticked consent, shaming choices and difficult cancellation. Show true stock and full cost, and make "no" as easy as "yes".

Do influencers have to disclose paid promotions?

They should clearly disclose material connections such as payment, gifted products or family and work links. Follow current ASCI and consumer-law guidance in India, or local rules where the audience lives.
