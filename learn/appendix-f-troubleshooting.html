🩺 Troubleshooting

Appendix F · By Vicky Prasad Mahato · Updated 2026-11-23

First ask: Did tracking, dates, offer, stock, season or definitions change?

SymptomLikely causesFirst fix
Traffic, no salesmismatch, cost, trust, broken checkoutwatch five people buy; split source/device

Test has no winnersmall sample/effectestimate interval; keep learning
Discount boosts orders, loses profitmargin/cannibalizationcontribution and normal buyer comparison
AI output invents factsmissing evidence/controlapproved fact IDs and human check
Automation sent twiceretry without deduplicationevent ID and suppression

The Five-Step Diagnosis

  1. Confirm the number.
  2. Locate where it changed.
  3. List competing causes.
  4. Run the cheapest decisive check.
  5. Change one main cause and record it.

⚠️ If privacy, unsafe claims or uncontrolled spending is involved, pause the affected activity first.
