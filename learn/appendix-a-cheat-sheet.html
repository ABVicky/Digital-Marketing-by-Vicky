📋 Appendix A: Cheat Sheet

The 10-Second Plan

Who + situation + problem + offer + proof + channel + action + measure + limit

Formulas

MetricFormula
CTRclicks ÷ impressions
CPCspend ÷ clicks
CPMspend ÷ impressions × 1,000
Conversion rateconversions ÷ visitors (or stated base)
CPAspend ÷ conversions
AOVrevenue ÷ orders
ROASattributed revenue ÷ ad spend
Contribution/ordernet price − variable costs
Break-even CPAcontribution/order
Break-even ROASnet price ÷ contribution/order
Target CPAcontribution − required amount left
Marginal CPAadded spend ÷ added conversions
CACacquisition cost ÷ new customers
Relative lift(variant − control) ÷ control

Channel Picker

SituationTry first
Urgent local needBusiness Profile, local search
Existing search demandSEO, search ads
New visual productshort video, paid social
Existing customers with permissionemail or WhatsApp
Narrow B2B marketpartnerships, useful outreach, LinkedIn, search
High trust neededreviews, experts, demonstrations

Page Check

  • [ ] What, who and action are clear
  • [ ] Page matches ad/search
  • [ ] Price and important terms are visible
  • [ ] Proof is true
  • [ ] Objections answered
  • [ ] Mobile, speed and keyboard tested
  • [ ] Conversion tested once

Campaign Check

  • [ ] Contribution and loss limit known
  • [ ] Audience and exclusions appropriate
  • [ ] Creative ideas are truly different
  • [ ] Claims, rights and disclosure approved
  • [ ] UTMs valid
  • [ ] Lead/order response ready
  • [ ] Review rule written

Red Flags

Guaranteed rankings · fake scarcity · unowned accounts · adding every platform's conversions together · optimizing for likes when sales are needed · changing five things at once · sending without permission · scaling because average ROAS looks good.
