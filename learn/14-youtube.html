▶️ YouTube Marketing

Module 14 · By Vicky Prasad Mahato · Updated 2026-11-23

🟠 Stage 3 · Social, Email & Ads · ⏱️ ~4 hours

A video called "How to centre clay when your hands shake" has 4,300 views after two years. That sounds small. But 40 people booked after watching it, and it still brings two bookings a month without another rupee. Most social posts are fireworks. A good YouTube video is a lamp.

🔑 In short: YouTube marketing means making videos that answer questions people search for, with an honest title and thumbnail, a quick start and one relevant next step. Good topics keep bringing viewers and customers long after publishing.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • Position a channel and choose topics.
  • Write titles, thumbnails and scripts that keep their promise.
  • Use search, Shorts, playlists and chapters.
  • Read analytics and connect videos to business.

1. Two Ways People Find Videos

RouteViewer's mindWhat helps
Search"I have a question"clear topic, direct answer, accurate title
Suggested/Home"This looks interesting"curiosity, strong thumbnail, satisfaction

A new business channel often starts with search-style topics, because demand is already clear. Over time, stories and comparisons can reach wider audiences.

2. Channel Promise

We help {{viewer}} {{make progress}} with {{type of video}},
without {{common difficulty}}.

We help beginners make and choose ceramics with short tested demonstrations, without needing a studio full of tools.

❌ Weak channel

Company events, festival wishes, TV ads, one tutorial from 2021.

✅ Strong channel

Playlist 1: Start pottery. Playlist 2: Choose tableware.
Playlist 3: We test it. Playlist 4: Fix common mistakes.

Setup

  • [ ] Brand account with business ownership
  • [ ] Banner states the promise
  • [ ] About section, links and contact
  • [ ] Trailer for new viewers
  • [ ] Four playlists by viewer job
  • [ ] Two-step verification

3. Topic Research

  1. Search autocomplete: "pottery for beginners ...".
  2. Comments on your and others' videos.
  3. Support and class questions.
  4. Search Console queries needing a demonstration.
  5. Competitor videos that are old, unclear or incomplete.

Choose topics where you can show something others can't.

4. Promise: Title + Thumbnail

They work together. Don't repeat the same words.

❌ Weak

Title: Pottery Video Episode 7 | Mitti Studio
Thumbnail: logo and small text

✅ Strong

Title: How to Centre Clay When Your Hands Shake (3 Fixes)
Thumbnail: wobbling clay close-up, words "It's not your hands"

Write 10 titles and sketch three thumbnails before filming. If you can't make an honest, interesting promise, the topic may be weak.

5. Script for Attention and Trust

0:00 Show the problem or result
0:10 Say what they'll be able to do
0:20 Start the first useful step
...  One step = one demonstration + one common mistake
End  Recap, then one relevant next video or action

Cut the long greeting. Say who you are when credibility helps: "I've taught this mistake to 300 beginners" (if true).

Retention tools that are honest

  • Show the end result early.
  • Change visual when the idea changes.
  • Number the steps.
  • Answer objections at the moment they appear.
  • Remove repeated explanation.

7. Shorts and Long Videos

ShortsLong form
Best jobdiscovery, single momentdepth, trust, search
Viewerswipingchose to watch
Next steprelated longer videopage, playlist, email

Don't simply chop a long video. A Short needs its own opening and payoff. Judge whether Shorts viewers go on to do anything you care about.

8. Business Integration

  1. Put a relevant video on product or service pages when it answers an objection.
  2. Link each video to one matching page.
  3. Use a UTM in descriptions.
  4. Ask enquiries "Which video helped?"
  5. Send videos through email and support.

Mitti embeds "Microwave test" beside the mug, and support sends it to customers who ask. One video now serves search, sales and service.

9. Monetization Isn't the Business Plan

YouTube's Partner Program rules change. Announcements in 2026 include updated terms and higher Shorts requirements for some revenue from 1 February 2027. Check the official eligibility page. For a business, one profitable customer may be worth more than years of ad revenue.

💻 Tech Lens

Use the YouTube Analytics export or API to join video ID, traffic source, views, watch time and UTM sessions. Build a table by topic cluster rather than one video. Add VideoObject structured data only on pages where the video is really present, and keep fields accurate.

🏋️ Exercises

Exercise 14.1: Improve a promise

"Fitness Motivation #12" for a trainer who shows desk workers how to relieve neck stiffness.

View Sample

Title: 5-Minute Neck Routine for Desk Workers (No Equipment). Thumbnail: person at desk, hand on neck, words "Between meetings". Include suitable safety guidance and avoid treatment promises.

Exercise 14.2: Diagnose

Video A: CTR 2%, average 70% viewed. Video B: CTR 11%, average 18% viewed. What next?

View Solution

A delivers but the promise isn't attracting clicks: test title/thumbnail. B attracts but disappoints: align the promise and improve the first minute. Check traffic sources and length before concluding.

Exercise 14.3: Make one

Publish a three-to-five-minute demonstration with chapters and corrected captions.

❓ Common Questions

How do I get more views on YouTube?

Choose topics with real demand, write the title and thumbnail before filming, show the problem or result at once and keep the promise. Low clicks point to the promise; early drops point to the opening.

Do YouTube tags matter?

Only a little. The topic, title, thumbnail, viewer satisfaction and a clear description matter far more. Use tags for common misspellings if useful and don't spend long on them.

Should a business make Shorts or long videos?

Use Shorts for discovery and one strong moment. Use longer videos for depth, trust and search. Check whether Shorts viewers go on to watch, visit or enquire before making them the main effort.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Topic and promise come before filming.
  • Search topics build lasting assets.
  • Title and thumbnail make a promise; the video must keep it.
  • Connect each video to one business step.
