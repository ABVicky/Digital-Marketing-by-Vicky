## 30-Day Practice Plan

| Days | Task |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | choose business, lab, read 00–01, register or review a site (04) |
| 4–7 | five interviews, position |
| 8–10 | unit economics and strategy |
| 11–13 | landing page and usability test |
| 14–16 | three pieces of copy, design or video |
| 17–19 | SEO/local audit and page fix |
| 20–22 | social or email plan |
| 23–24 | search ads or paid social plan |
| 25–26 | event/UTM plan and one GTM test |
| 27 | conversion hypothesis |
| 28 | privacy and claim review |
| 29 | 90-day plan |
| 30 | present and take final exam |

The 30-day plan gives you a first pass, not mastery. Repeat the cycle using real evidence.
