## 3. AI Search and Answer Platforms

| Where you want to appear | What you can actually do | Register or check |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | claim an eligible business (beta), keep pages crawlable, earn independent mentions | [chatgpt.com/business-hub](https://chatgpt.com/business-hub), [announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) |
| Google AI Overviews/AI Mode | index useful pages; normal SEO | [Google guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) |
| Gemini shopping surfaces | accurate Merchant Center data where supported | [merchants.google.com](https://merchants.google.com/) |
| Microsoft Copilot | Bing Webmaster Tools, AI Performance, Bing Places | [bing.com/webmasters](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/) |
| Perplexity | crawlable pages; Merchant Program only if eligible | check current program terms |
| Claude and other assistants | accurate public pages and trusted mentions | no general business registration found in our check |

> ⚠️ Nobody can sell you a guaranteed place in an AI answer. Claiming a profile helps keep facts correct; it doesn't guarantee a recommendation.
