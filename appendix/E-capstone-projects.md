# 🏆 Appendix E — Capstone Projects

Use a business you own, written permission from an organization, or clearly labelled fictional data. Paid spend is optional in every project.

## Levels

| Level | Standard |
|---|---|
| Bronze | complete, reasoned plan |
| Silver | working asset and tested process |
| Gold | real users/data with permission, comparison and limits |

## 1. 👔 Customer-to-Campaign Project

Interview five people, choose one segment, position the offer, build a landing page and three creative ideas.

**Deliver:** coded notes, job, economics, page, campaign brief, next test.

**Success:** strangers can explain the offer; claims have evidence; budget has a limit.

## 2. 👔 Local Business Growth Plan

Audit discovery, profile, reviews, response and visit experience. Make a 90-day plan.

**Deliver:** baseline, checklist, two content pieces, review process, report template.

**Success:** all public facts verified and no review incentives or keyword-stuffed names.

## 3. 👔 Lifecycle Program

Plan signup, welcome, purchase, repeat, preference and stop.

**Deliver:** flow map, eight messages, permission record, suppression and test plan.

**Success:** a new user can enter, act, leave and receive no conflicting messages.

## 4. 💻 Measurement System

Build a measurement plan, data layer, test script, UTM rules and one report.

**Deliver:** event documentation, test evidence, reconciliation, privacy map.

**Success:** test orders are counted once; failed payments don't count; numbers trace to source.

## 5. 💻 Technical SEO Improvement

Crawl your own site or one you may test, fix a meaningful issue and measure again.

**Deliver:** issue evidence, page purpose, change, rollback, before/after technical check.

**Success:** site works, indexing intent is correct, and ranking claims state limits.

## 6. 💻 Reliable Lead Automation

Build form → validate → CRM → notify → follow-up → opt-out.

**Deliver:** code/workflow, tests, duplicate handling, failure queue, runbook.

**Success:** duplicate event creates one lead; invalid signature is rejected; opted-out person isn't messaged.

## Presentation Rubric

| Area | Points |
|---|---|
| Customer evidence | 20 |
| Strategy/economics | 20 |
| Execution | 20 |
| Measurement | 20 |
| Ethics and clear limits | 20 |

Present the problem before the tools. Show one thing that failed and how you handled it.
