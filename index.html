Digital Marketing by Vicky: Zero to Pro

Digital marketing is the work of helping the right people discover, trust, buy and keep using something through websites, search, social networks, messaging and apps. Good results come from matching the message to the customer's stage and judging profit, not likes.

Common questions

How do I start learning digital marketing with no experience?

Interview a few customers, learn the basic numbers (conversion rate, CPA, contribution), make one landing page, then practise one channel for several weeks. Module order 01, 02, 03 and 05 gives you that base before you spend anything on ads. Read Welcome & How to Use This Guide

What are the main types of digital marketing?

The main types are SEO, search ads, social media, paid social ads, content, email and WhatsApp, creator partnerships, and analytics with conversion work. They divide into paid, owned and earned media, and they usually work together rather than alone. Read How Digital Marketing Works

How do I calculate break-even CPA?

Subtract product, packing, shipping, payment fees and expected returns from the net price. A ₹1,800 order with ₹990 variable costs leaves ₹810, so break-even CPA is ₹810. To keep ₹250 per order, target a CPA of ₹560. Read Strategy & Unit Economics

How do I do keyword research?

Begin with customer phrases, add autocomplete, Search Console and tool ideas, group queries that deserve the same answer, then rank by relevance, business value and the original evidence you can offer. Assign one main page per group. Read SEO: Keywords & On-Page

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) aims for visibility in search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) aims to be the direct answer. GEO (generative engine optimization) aims to be used or cited in AI answers. Google treats the last two as part of SEO. Read Local SEO & AI Search

How much does Google Ads cost?

Cost depends on the auction, intent and competition. Estimate expected CPA by dividing CPC by conversion rate: ₹18 ÷ 4% is ₹450. Compare it with your contribution and set a total loss limit before launch. Read Google Ads
