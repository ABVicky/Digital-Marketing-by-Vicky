# 👥 Module 13 — Facebook (Organic)

> 🟠 Stage 3 · Social, Email & Ads · ⏱️ ~3 hours

Meera nearly deleted the Facebook Page. "It's only relatives there." Then someone in a neighbourhood group asked, *"Where can I book a birthday activity for twelve children?"* Three members tagged Mitti, and the party booking covered a month's studio rent. Facebook's value was never in the Page posts. It was in the group.

> 🔑 **In short:** Organic Facebook marketing works best through useful answers in local and interest groups, well-run events, quick replies and original content. A Page mainly gives people facts and reassurance, because most followers won't see any single post.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Decide whether Facebook deserves your time.
- Set up a Page that answers and reassures.
- Use groups, events and Messenger respectfully.
- Publish original content and measure it.

## 1. Does Facebook Fit?

| Good signs | Weaker signs |
|---|---|
| local service or venue | audience says they rarely use it |
| parents, hobby groups, community events | product needs fast trend culture |
| customers ask in groups | no one to answer messages |
| you run Meta ads and need a credible Page | you want guaranteed unpaid reach |

Ask ten customers, "Where did you last ask for a recommendation?" Their answer beats any national statistic.

Third-party studies commonly put organic Page reach at a small share of followers. Methods differ, so use your own data. Either way, plan as if most followers won't see a given post.

## 2. Page, Profile or Group?

| | Page | Profile | Group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Represents | business | person | shared interest |
| Good for | information, ads, reviews | personal network | conversation |
| Access | roles | one person | admins and moderators |
| Avoid | pretending to be a friend | using a fake person for business | constant selling |

Use a Page for the business. Give staff roles instead of passwords, and keep at least two trusted people with full control.

## 3. Page Setup

- [ ] Real name, category, hours, address and service area
- [ ] Profile and cover readable on mobile
- [ ] Button matches the main action (book, call, message)
- [ ] Short description with customer, offer and place
- [ ] Policies and website linked
- [ ] Messages answered by a named owner
- [ ] Two-factor login
- [ ] Pinned post: start here

❌ **Weak About**
```text
We are a leading provider of innovative ceramic solutions.
```

✅ **Strong About**
```text
Pottery classes and handmade tableware in Kolkata. Beginner, couples
and children's sessions, up to 8 people. Message us for dates; we
reply 10 AM to 6 PM, Tuesday to Sunday.
```

## 4. What to Publish

| Job | Idea |
|---|---|
| Useful locally | monsoon-safe weekend plans nearby |
| Proof | children's party photos with guardian permission |
| Conversation | "What did your grandmother use for pickles?" |
| Event | open clay demo, Saturday |
| Answer | price, parking, age, timing |
| Video | making, testing, teaching |

Ask questions people can answer from life. "Like if you agree" is engagement bait and tells you nothing.

### Originality matters

Meta says it prioritizes **original** content and reduces reach for unoriginal material. Read its [2026 post on rewarding original creators](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/). Re-uploading someone's clip with a border is both poor marketing and possible infringement. Film your own, or add real commentary with permission.

### Links

When you want a website visit, explain why the page is worth it and compare results of link posts and native posts in your own data. Don't hide the purpose or use misleading previews.

## 5. Groups: The Real Community

### As a member

1. Read the rules.
2. Help for two weeks before mentioning your work.
3. Answer completely, without requiring a click.
4. Disclose your connection.
5. Post offers only where allowed.

❌ **Weak**
```text
Best pottery!! Call now 98XXXXXXXX!! (posted in 40 groups)
```

✅ **Strong**
```text
For twelve 8-year-olds, plan 90 minutes: 15 to show, 45 to make, 15 to
decorate, 15 to clean up. Air-dry clay avoids the second trip. I run a
studio (Mitti), so I'm biased, but the same plan works at home.
```

The second answer may be found by parents for years.

### Running your own group

Start one only if members gain from *each other*. "Kolkata Beginner Potters" can work. "Mitti Studio Offers" is a mailing list with extra steps.

| Set-up | Example |
|---|---|
| Purpose | help beginners practise and share |
| Entry questions | level? what do you want to make? |
| Rules | kind, on-topic, no spam, ask before selling |
| Weekly rhythm | Mon question, Wed tip, Sat showcase |
| Moderation | two people, written removal rule |
| First 30 members | personally invited |

Judge it by members helping members, not by member count.

## 6. Events

1. Create with exact date, place, price and what to bring.
2. Use one clear image.
3. Invite relevant people and partners.
4. Post answers to repeated questions.
5. Remind responders with useful information.
6. Record actual attendance.
7. Share photos with permission, and the next date.

"Interested" isn't "attending". Track the ratio so you can plan seats.

```text
Show-up rate = people who attended / confirmed bookings
```

## 7. Messenger and Comments

Prepare saved replies for price, timing, place, age and delivery, then personalize.

```text
Thanks for asking about the 12 December class. Beginners are welcome;
it's ₹1,500 including firing. Parking is on the next lane. Two seats
are open. Would morning or afternoon suit you?
```

Business messaging has time-window and content rules that change. Check current policy before automated or promotional messages, and always offer a human.

### Handling a public complaint

| Minute/hour | Action |
|---|---|
| First look | verify, screenshot, check order |
| Promptly | acknowledge, one sentence, no blame |
| Next | move details to private message |
| Resolved | brief public update if helpful |
| Weekly | fix the cause |

## 8. Reviews and Recommendations

Ask everyone for honest feedback, reply to fair criticism, and report content that breaks rules instead of arguing. Never use staff accounts to review the business.

## 9. Measure

| Question | Metric |
|---|---|
| Seen by whom? | reach, local share |
| Conversation? | meaningful comments, shares |
| Service? | messages answered, median time |
| Community? | active members, member-to-member replies |
| Business? | bookings with "Facebook group" as source |

Ask "How did you hear about us?" in the booking form. Group recommendations often arrive as direct or branded search visits.

> 👔 **Two hours a week:** 30 min useful answers in groups, 45 min two original posts, 30 min messages, 15 min review.

> 💻 **Set-up hygiene:** manage Page, ad account, pixel/dataset and domain in one business portfolio owned by the client. Review people, partners and apps quarterly.

## 10. From Organic to Paid

A post that earns real questions is a candidate for an ad. Boosting is the quick version, while Ads Manager offers more control (Module 19). Never boost simply because a button offers it.

## 🛠️ Practical Lab: Build a Facebook Page, Help in Groups and Run an Event

**You will finish with:** a complete Page, ten useful contributions and one event. Time: 4 hours plus a week of participation.

### Register

| Asset | Open | You need |
|---|---|---|
| Page | [facebook.com/pages/create](https://www.facebook.com/pages/create) | personal profile and business facts |
| Business portfolio | [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) | secure business email |
| Group | Facebook → Groups → Create | clear reason for members to talk |
| Event | Page → Events → Create event | place/date/price/capacity |

### Page, step by step

1. Create Page with the real business name, precise category and short description.
2. Add profile photo, cover, phone, website, location/hours or service area, WhatsApp if monitored, and action button.
3. Choose **New Pages/Professional dashboard** tools available to you.
4. Add two trusted people by role; don't share your profile password.
5. In Business portfolio, add the Page through **Accounts → Pages** or current equivalent. Confirm ownership.
6. Write a pinned post: who you help, how to buy/book, price range, proof and support hours.
7. Configure inbox response, saved replies and a human handoff.
8. View the Page in a private window and on mobile.

### Find groups the right way

1. Search `your city + parents`, `hobby + city`, `buy gifts`, or your profession.
2. Read rules, recent posts, moderator activity and whether businesses may participate.
3. Join three. Spend the first week helping, not posting.
4. Keep a log: `question, full answer, connection disclosed, rule checked, result`.
5. Link only when rules allow and the page genuinely adds detail.

### Create your own group only if...

You can name 30 people who would help **each other**. Use entry questions, a welcome post, rules, a weekly member prompt, moderation and a backup admin. If your plan is only announcements, use email instead.

### Event, step by step

1. Page → Events → Create. Select in-person or online.
2. Add image, exact date/time zone, location/access, price, capacity, cancellation and booking link.
3. Put one clear thing to bring and one question people ask.
4. Publish; share to your own Page and partner places with permission.
5. Reply to Interested people without treating interest as a booking.
6. Use confirmed booking sheet, reminder and waitlist.
7. After event: thank, ask for honest feedback, share only permitted photos, record show-up rate.

### Practice task

Find three genuine questions about beginner activities. Answer each so fully that the person could act without clicking. Disclose your connection if your work is mentioned.

### If it goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Page name rejected | match real business, proof and policy |
| Group removes post | read rule, apologize if needed; don't argue by reposting |
| Event interest but no bookings | move action, price and date to first lines |
| Fake support comments | hide/report, pin official contact |

### Proof to save

- [ ] Complete Page and secured roles
- [ ] Ten group contributions with rules checked
- [ ] Event page and attendance calculation
- [ ] Three customer questions learned

## ❓ Common Questions

### Is Facebook still useful for business?

Yes, if your customers ask for recommendations in groups, attend events or message businesses there. It suits many local services, parents and hobby communities. Ask customers where they last sought a recommendation before investing time.

### Should I make a Facebook Page or a Group?

Use a Page to represent the business. Start a Group only when members benefit from talking to each other. If you only plan to post offers, email will probably work better.

### How do I promote a business in Facebook groups without spamming?

Read the rules, give complete answers for a couple of weeks, disclose your connection whenever you mention your business and post offers only where allowed. Never paste the same advert into many groups.

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- Groups and local usefulness often matter more than Page posts.
- Disclose your connection when recommending yourself.
- Original content is safer and more valuable.
- Answering is marketing.
