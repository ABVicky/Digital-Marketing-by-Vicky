# 📱 Module 11 — Social Media Strategy & Influencers

> 🟠 Stage 3 · Social, Email & Ads · ⏱️ ~4 hours

A social feed is a crowded festival. Walking in and shouting your price rarely works. Demonstrating something interesting, helping people nearby and remembering their names often does.

> 🔑 **In short:** A social media strategy chooses one main platform where your customers already spend time, a few repeatable content jobs (reach, teach, trust, community, convert), a realistic routine, and a path from attention to enquiries or sales.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Choose platforms by audience and format.
- Plan short videos and community work.
- Build a creator brief.
- Measure beyond likes.

---

## 1. Choose One Main and One Support Platform

| Strength | Possible fit |
|---|---|
| Visual discovery, community | Instagram |
| Search and lasting video | YouTube |
| Professional expertise | LinkedIn |
| Local groups, older communities | Facebook |
| Conversation and news | X, Threads |
| Visual planning | Pinterest |

Confirm with customer interviews. Your buyer's habits matter more than platform totals.

### Write a one-page social strategy

```text
Business outcome:
Customer and situation:
Why social (and why this platform):
Promise of our account:
Four pillars:
Two formats we can sustain:
Community role:
Conversion path:
Measures:
We will not:
Review date:
```

Mitti's promise: *"We show what handmade really looks like, including failures, so people can choose, gift and try pottery with confidence."*

### Use the platform modules as a menu

| If your buyer… | Start with |
|---|---|
| discovers through visuals | Module 12, Instagram |
| asks local groups | Module 13, Facebook |
| searches "how to" | Module 14, YouTube |
| buys for a company | Module 15, LinkedIn |
| discusses news or tech | Module 16, X and Threads |

## 2. Mix Content by Job

| Job | Mitti post |
|---|---|
| Reach | cutting a lopsided bowl in half |
| Trust | parcel drop test |
| Teach | choose a mug size |
| Community | vote on the next glaze |
| Convert | Saturday seats, price and link |

A feed of offers gives people little reason to return. A feed with no offers makes it hard to buy.

### A balanced month

| Job | Share to try | Posts in a 16-post month |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | 30% | 5 |
| Teach | 25% | 4 |
| Trust | 20% | 3 |
| Community | 15% | 2 |
| Convert | 10% | 2 |

This is only a starting mix. A new account may need more reach; a fully booked studio may need more care and community.

### One idea, five natural versions

| Place | Drop-test idea becomes |
|---|---|
| Instagram | fast vertical test |
| YouTube | full method and 40 drops |
| LinkedIn | lesson about lost quality and cost |
| Facebook group | answer to "How do I post fragile gifts?" |
| X | four results and limits |

## 3. Short-Video Structure

```text
0-2 s   Show the tension or result
3-15 s  Prove or demonstrate
15-25 s Explain what matters
End     One natural next step
```

Timings are a drafting guide.

❌ **Weak opening**
```text
Hi everyone, welcome back to our page. Today we will talk about...
```

✅ **Strong opening**
```text
We dropped this gift box from table height. Let's see if the mugs lived.
```

Film in readable light, get clear audio, add captions, keep key text away from app buttons and respect music licences.

### A 20-video test

Make five subjects × four openings. Keep the main idea steady within each subject. Look for patterns over several posts; one hit may be luck.

## 4. Captions and Social Search

Use the words customers search for. State the subject in the caption, speech and on-screen text where natural. Hashtags can label; they can't rescue an uninteresting post.

## 5. Community Is Marketing

- Reply to useful questions.
- Turn repeated questions into content.
- Escalate refund, safety and privacy issues.
- Moderate using public rules.
- Thank people for creating content and ask permission before reuse.

Crisis rule: pause scheduled promotions, verify facts, choose one owner, acknowledge, update and document. Deleting fair criticism can make it worse.

### Community roles

| Role | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Host | sets the tone, introduces members |
| Teacher | gives complete help |
| Listener | notes patterns |
| Moderator | protects people |
| Customer service | solves orders |

Decide who plays each role and during which hours. "The intern handles comments" is not a policy.

### Moderation rules people can understand

```text
Disagree with ideas, not people.
No hate, threats, private information or spam.
Complaints are welcome; we'll answer and may move details private.
We remove posts that break these rules and note why.
```

## 6. Creators

Choose on audience fit, past work, comment quality and values. Follower count alone is weak.

| Check | Question |
|---|---|
| Audience | Where are they and what do they buy? |
| Credibility | Would this recommendation make sense? |
| Evidence | Can they share real recent results? |
| Safety | Any conflict or harmful conduct? |
| Rights | Where and how long may we use the video? |

### Brief

```text
Objective and customer:
One true message:
Claims allowed / prohibited:
Mandatory demo:
Creative control:
Deliverables and dates:
Fee, usage rights and exclusivity:
Disclosure wording and placement:
Tracking link/code:
Approval and revision limit:
```

> ⚠️ Payment, gifted products, discounts, employment and family links can be material connections. Disclosures must be clear and hard to miss. Check current ASCI guidance and consumer-protection rules in India, or the law where the audience lives.

### Influencer economics before outreach

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fee | ₹10,000 |
| Product + shipping | ₹1,800 |
| Usage rights (3 months) | ₹3,000 |
| **Total** | **₹14,800** |

At ₹810 contribution, break-even is 14,800 ÷ 810 = 18.3, so **19 added orders**. Ask whether this person's audience could plausibly bring them, or whether the reusable video justifies part of the cost.

### Negotiate fairly

❌ **Weak offer**
```text
We'll give exposure and a mug. Please make 3 Reels.
```

✅ **Strong offer**
```text
One 30-45 s video testing our gift box, ₹{{fee}} plus the set. You keep
creative control within factual limits. We'd like organic repost and
three months of ad use as a separate ₹{{rights_fee}}. One factual
revision. Paid-partnership label required. Payment within 7 days.
```

Pay for work, rights and exclusivity separately. Confirm tax and invoice details in writing.

## 7. Measure

| Level | Metrics |
|---|---|
| Attention | reach, hold rate, completion |
| Response | saves, shares, replies, qualified visits |
| Business | enquiries, sales, contribution |
| Learning | themes, objections, product ideas |

```text
Influencer cost per order = (fee + product + shipping + ads) / orders
```

Codes miss people who later search. Links and surveys miss others. Use several imperfect signals together.

> 👔 **Weekly review:** keep one idea, improve one, stop one.

> 💻 **Link naming:** use lowercase UTMs and a controlled sheet. Never put personal data in links.

## 8. Social Customer Care

| Type | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Safety/privacy | immediate escalation | Meera |
| Payment/order | same working day | support |
| Product question | within stated hours | studio |
| Feedback | acknowledge and log | marketing |
| Abuse | document, hide/report as policy | moderator |

Publish realistic hours. A fast answer that's wrong creates two problems.

## 9. User-Generated Content

1. Ask with the exact asset, places and duration.
2. Credit as requested.
3. Don't alter meaning.
4. Keep proof of permission.
5. Stop using when the agreement ends.

```text
Your photo shows the glaze beautifully. May we use it on our product
page and Instagram for 12 months, credited to {{handle}}? You can say
no, and it won't affect anything.
```

Tagging a brand isn't automatic permission for advertising.

## 10. Trend Decisions

Ask five questions:

1. Does our customer recognize it?
2. Does it help our message?
3. Are rights and context safe?
4. Can we make it today without harming planned work?
5. Would it still make sense next week?

Two "no" answers: skip it. Tragedies, protests and sensitive identities are not posting opportunities.

## 11. Diagnose an Account

| Symptom | Evidence to inspect | Likely test |
|---|---|---|
| Low reach | recent 20 posts by job and opening | new topics, clearer opening |
| Reach, no follows | profile match | profile promise and pinned posts |
| Follows, no sales | buyer share, path | useful conversion post, page |
| Many likes, no saves | depth | tutorial or comparison |
| Negative comments | theme and truth | fix product/policy |
| One person makes all results | dependence | new series and owned list |

### Social listening sheet

```text
Date | place | exact phrase | need | sentiment | product area | action | owner
```

Copy only what you need and avoid collecting personal profiles.

## 12. A 30-Day Operating System

| Cadence | Task |
|---|---|
| Daily, 25 min | replies, one useful interaction, errors |
| Monday | customer question and weekly plan |
| Tuesday | film and design |
| Wednesday | edit and check |
| Thursday | schedule, collaborations |
| Friday | results and learning log |
| Month end | keep, improve, stop |

### Reporting example

```text
Goal: 25 workshop bookings. Result: 18 confirmed; 7 said Instagram,
4 group recommendation, 7 unknown/other.
Learning: "first-time fear" posts had 2.1x profile visits per reach
compared with our beauty posts (12 posts, varied dates; not conclusive).
Decision: four more fear-answer posts, two new openings.
```

These are example figures. Notice the limits: sources are self-reported and the comparison isn't an experiment.

## 13. Account Safety

- Two-factor login; recovery codes in a password manager.
- No shared personal passwords.
- Quarterly access review.
- Official support links only.
- A process for hacked accounts: secure email, change access, remove unknown apps, notify customers on another channel, preserve evidence.

> 🔑 Social accounts are rented space. Invite interested people to email, a community you run, or a useful tool on your site.

## ❓ Common Questions

### Which social media platform is best for business?

The best one is where your buyers discover, ask or decide. Visual products often suit Instagram, how-to topics suit YouTube, B2B suits LinkedIn and local services benefit from Facebook groups. Ask ten customers before choosing.

### How do I choose an influencer?

Check audience fit, comment quality, past relevant work and values. Calculate break-even first: a ₹14,800 total cost at ₹810 contribution needs 19 added orders. Agree on rights, disclosure and claims in writing.

### How many times a week should I post?

Post as often as you can stay useful. Two strong pieces a week for six months beat daily posts that stop in three weeks. Compare posts with the same job to decide whether to do more.
