# ▶️ Module 14 — YouTube Marketing

> 🟠 Stage 3 · Social, Email & Ads · ⏱️ ~4 hours

A video called "How to centre clay when your hands shake" has 4,300 views after two years. That sounds small. But 40 people booked after watching it, and it still brings two bookings a month without another rupee. Most social posts are fireworks. A good YouTube video is a lamp.

> 🔑 **In short:** YouTube marketing means making videos that answer questions people search for, with an honest title and thumbnail, a quick start and one relevant next step. Good topics keep bringing viewers and customers long after publishing.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Position a channel and choose topics.
- Write titles, thumbnails and scripts that keep their promise.
- Use search, Shorts, playlists and chapters.
- Read analytics and connect videos to business.

## 1. Two Ways People Find Videos

| Route | Viewer's mind | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| Search | "I have a question" | clear topic, direct answer, accurate title |
| Suggested/Home | "This looks interesting" | curiosity, strong thumbnail, satisfaction |

A new business channel often starts with search-style topics, because demand is already clear. Over time, stories and comparisons can reach wider audiences.

## 2. Channel Promise

```text
We help {{viewer}} {{make progress}} with {{type of video}},
without {{common difficulty}}.
```

*We help beginners make and choose ceramics with short tested demonstrations, without needing a studio full of tools.*

❌ **Weak channel**
```text
Company events, festival wishes, TV ads, one tutorial from 2021.
```

✅ **Strong channel**
```text
Playlist 1: Start pottery. Playlist 2: Choose tableware.
Playlist 3: We test it. Playlist 4: Fix common mistakes.
```

### Setup

- [ ] Brand account with business ownership
- [ ] Banner states the promise
- [ ] About section, links and contact
- [ ] Trailer for new viewers
- [ ] Four playlists by viewer job
- [ ] Two-step verification

## 3. Topic Research

1. Search autocomplete: "pottery for beginners ...".
2. Comments on your and others' videos.
3. Support and class questions.
4. Search Console queries needing a demonstration.
5. Competitor videos that are old, unclear or incomplete.

| Idea | Demand | We have proof? | Business value | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Centre clay | 4 | 5 | 4 | 13 |
| Handmade vs factory mug | 3 | 5 | 5 | 13 |
| History of ceramics | 4 | 2 | 1 | 7 |
| Microwave test | 4 | 5 | 4 | 13 |

Choose topics where you can *show* something others can't.

## 4. Promise: Title + Thumbnail

They work together. Don't repeat the same words.

❌ **Weak**
```text
Title: Pottery Video Episode 7 | Mitti Studio
Thumbnail: logo and small text
```

✅ **Strong**
```text
Title: How to Centre Clay When Your Hands Shake (3 Fixes)
Thumbnail: wobbling clay close-up, words "It's not your hands"
```

Write 10 titles and sketch three thumbnails *before* filming. If you can't make an honest, interesting promise, the topic may be weak.

## 5. Script for Attention and Trust

```text
0:00 Show the problem or result
0:10 Say what they'll be able to do
0:20 Start the first useful step
...  One step = one demonstration + one common mistake
End  Recap, then one relevant next video or action
```

Cut the long greeting. Say who you are when credibility helps: "I've taught this mistake to 300 beginners" (if true).

### Retention tools that are honest

- Show the end result early.
- Change visual when the idea changes.
- Number the steps.
- Answer objections at the moment they appear.
- Remove repeated explanation.

## 6. Publish Properly

| Field | Use |
|---|---|
| Description | first two lines answer "what and for whom"; then links and resources |
| Chapters | timestamps starting at 0:00 |
| Subtitles | correct the automatic captions |
| Cards/end screen | one next video |
| Playlist | place in a learning path |
| Pinned comment | correction or helpful resource |
| Tags | minor help; don't obsess |

```text
0:00 Why the clay wobbles
0:42 Fix 1: anchor your elbows
1:55 Fix 2: water, not force
3:10 Fix 3: slow the wheel
4:20 Practice plan
```

Use disclosure settings for paid promotion and for realistic altered or synthetic content when required. Follow [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/).

## 7. Shorts and Long Videos

| | Shorts | Long form |
|---|---|---|
| Best job | discovery, single moment | depth, trust, search |
| Viewer | swiping | chose to watch |
| Next step | related longer video | page, playlist, email |

Don't simply chop a long video. A Short needs its own opening and payoff. Judge whether Shorts viewers go on to do anything you care about.

## 8. Business Integration

1. Put a relevant video on product or service pages when it answers an objection.
2. Link each video to **one** matching page.
3. Use a UTM in descriptions.
4. Ask enquiries "Which video helped?"
5. Send videos through email and support.

Mitti embeds "Microwave test" beside the mug, and support sends it to customers who ask. One video now serves search, sales and service.

## 9. Monetization Isn't the Business Plan

YouTube's Partner Program rules change. Announcements in 2026 include updated terms and higher Shorts requirements for some revenue from **1 February 2027**. Check the [official eligibility page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851). For a business, one profitable customer may be worth more than years of ad revenue.

## 10. Analytics That Matter

| Symptom | Likely area | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Few impressions | topic/demand, new channel | stronger topic, link from your own assets |
| Impressions, low CTR | title/thumbnail fit | new promise |
| CTR high, early drop | promise mismatch, slow start | deliver sooner |
| Good retention, no visits | no useful next step | one clear link |
| Visits, no booking | landing page/offer | Module 05 |

CTR depends on where the video is shown, so compare similar sources. Average view duration should be read with video length.

```text
Booking value per 1,000 views = attributed booking contribution / views x 1000
```

### 💻 Tech Lens
Use the YouTube Analytics export or API to join video ID, traffic source, views, watch time and UTM sessions. Build a table by *topic cluster* rather than one video. Add `VideoObject` structured data only on pages where the video is really present, and keep fields accurate.

## 11. YouTube Ads in One Page

| Goal | Approach |
|---|---|
| Show to relevant people | demo creative, in-feed or in-stream |
| Create demand | Demand Gen with strong image/video |
| Reach people who know you | permitted remarketing lists |

The skippable ad must make sense before the skip. Say the problem, show the proof, name the brand naturally and give one action. Measure incremental outcomes when the budget justifies it (Module 20).

## 🛠️ Practical Lab: Publish a Searchable YouTube Video

**You will finish with:** a business-owned channel and one helpful, captioned video. Time: 8 hours.

### Tools and registration

| Job | Tool | Open |
|---|---|---|
| Create/manage channel | YouTube Studio | [studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com/) |
| Topic interest | Google Trends, YouTube autocomplete | [trends.google.com](https://trends.google.com/) |
| Edit | VN, DaVinci Resolve | Module 07 |
| Audio | YouTube Audio Library | YouTube Studio → Audio Library |
| Thumbnail | Canva | [canva.com](https://www.canva.com/) |
| Ads | Google Ads | Module 18 |

### Create a business-owned channel

1. Sign in with the business Google account. Open YouTube → profile → **Create a channel**.
2. If multiple people will manage it, use a Brand Account where appropriate or Studio permissions. Don't share one password.
3. Studio → Customization: name, handle, image, banner, description, contact and links.
4. Studio → Settings → Permissions: add limited roles.
5. Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility: complete only needed verification.
6. Create four playlists: Start, Choose, Fix, Proof.

### Choose and film

1. List 20 real questions. Search each on YouTube and record date, promise, missing proof and comments.
2. Choose one you can demonstrate better.
3. Write ten titles and sketch three thumbnails before filming.
4. Script result → three steps → common mistake → limit → next action.
5. Film wide, medium, close. Record clear voice and one thumbnail image.
6. Edit out greeting and repeated explanation. Correct captions.

### Publish step by step

1. Studio → **Create → Upload videos**.
2. Title: plain outcome/question. Description: useful first two lines, resources, honest affiliate/sponsor disclosure.
3. Upload thumbnail; check at small size.
4. Choose playlist, audience setting (including made-for-kids accurately), language and licence.
5. Add chapters starting `0:00`.
6. Upload corrected captions.
7. Use paid-promotion and altered/synthetic-content settings when they apply.
8. Add end screen to one related video; add relevant card.
9. Set Private/Unlisted, watch once, then Public or Schedule.
10. Pin one useful resource. Embed the video on a matching page and link back with a UTM.

### Week-one analysis

Wait until enough people have seen it. Studio → Analytics:

| Tab | Ask |
|---|---|
| Reach | who was shown it, from where? |
| Engagement | where did they leave? |
| Audience | are they relevant/returning? |
| Comments | what is still unclear? |

Change title/thumbnail **or** opening in the next video, not both, if you want to learn.

### Practice task

Publish *"Three checks before buying a handmade mug"*. Show size in hand, base/wobble and care label. Link to a real size guide, not a discount.

### If it goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Custom thumbnail unavailable | check feature eligibility/verification |
| Copyright claim | read claim, remove/replace/dispute only with rights |
| No impressions | stronger search topic, links from site/email |
| Clicks but exits | deliver promise earlier |

### Proof to save

- [ ] Channel roles and public URL
- [ ] Title/thumbnail test
- [ ] Published video with captions/chapters
- [ ] One retention finding and one next-video change

## ❓ Common Questions

### How do I get more views on YouTube?

Choose topics with real demand, write the title and thumbnail before filming, show the problem or result at once and keep the promise. Low clicks point to the promise; early drops point to the opening.

### Do YouTube tags matter?

Only a little. The topic, title, thumbnail, viewer satisfaction and a clear description matter far more. Use tags for common misspellings if useful and don't spend long on them.

### Should a business make Shorts or long videos?

Use Shorts for discovery and one strong moment. Use longer videos for depth, trust and search. Check whether Shorts viewers go on to watch, visit or enquire before making them the main effort.

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- Topic and promise come before filming.
- Search topics build lasting assets.
- Title and thumbnail make a promise; the video must keep it.
- Connect each video to one business step.
