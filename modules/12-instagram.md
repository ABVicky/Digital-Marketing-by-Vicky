# 📸 Module 12 — Instagram Marketing

> 🟠 Stage 3 · Social, Email & Ads · ⏱️ ~4 hours

Mitti's most-liked post is a cat sleeping in a bowl. It reached 90,000 people and sold nothing. Its best-*selling* post reached 2,100: a plain carousel called "Which mug size fits your coffee?" Reach and usefulness are different jobs. A healthy account has both and knows which is which.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Set up a profile that converts visitors.
- Choose between Reels, carousels, Stories, Lives and DMs.
- Run a four-week content test.
- Turn attention into enquiries and sales without spamming.

---

## 1. Set Up the Profile

Use a professional account so you get insights and contact options. Choose *business* or *creator* based on current features, including music access.

| Element | Job | Mitti example |
|---|---|---|
| Name field | searchable words | Mitti Studio · Pottery Kolkata |
| Bio line 1 | who and what | Handmade mugs and weekend classes |
| Bio line 2 | proof | Max 8 per class · breakage replaced |
| Action | next step | Book a seat ↓ |
| Link | matching page | workshop page, with UTM |
| Highlights | answers | Classes, Prices, Reviews, Care, FAQ |
| Pinned posts | best first impression | start here, proof, popular product |

❌ **Weak bio**
```text
Dreamer. Creator. Making magic since 2021. DM for price.
```

✅ **Strong bio**
```text
Handmade mugs + beginner pottery classes, Kolkata
Max 8 people. Clay and firing included.
Shipping across India. Breakages replaced.
Book this weekend:
```

"DM for price" makes curious people work, and fills your inbox with questions a page could answer.

## 2. Pick the Right Format

| Format | Best job | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | being discovered | watch time, shares, saves |
| Carousel | teaching and comparing | saves, swipes, shares |
| Single image | announcement, beauty | comments, profile visits |
| Stories | daily trust and questions | replies, taps, exits |
| Live | demonstration and Q&A | questions, returning viewers |
| DM | decision help | replies, bookings |
| Collab post | borrowed audience | new relevant visitors |
| Broadcast channel | loyal updates | reactions, leaves |

Instagram's systems differ for Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels. Treat any "secret algorithm" list with caution. In practice, aim for three human reactions: *watched it, saved it, sent it to someone.*

### Hashtags

Instagram reportedly limited posts and Reels to **five hashtags** in December 2025. Use a few specific ones, and put your real keywords in the caption, spoken words and on-screen text, where people searching can find them. Check the current limit in the app.

## 3. The Content Matrix

Cross your pillars (Module 06) with formats.

| | Reel | Carousel | Story |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Choose** | size comparison in hand | mug-size guide | poll: 200 or 300 ml? |
| **Care** | tea-stain test | 5 care mistakes | quiz |
| **Gift** | pack a gift with me | gifts by budget | question box |
| **Make** | bowl collapses | tools explained | behind the kiln |
| **Trust** | drop test | customer photos (permitted) | reply to a review |

That's 15 ideas. Multiply by three openings each.

## 4. Reels That Earn Their Seconds

```text
Opening   show tension or result at once
Middle    deliver steps or proof, no filler
Payoff    the thing they waited for
Loop/End  natural finish or one next step
```

### Ten openings to adapt

1. "This isn't a crack."
2. "We dropped it so your courier can't surprise you."
3. "Most beginners press here. Don't."
4. "₹850 mug or ₹200 mug: what actually changes?"
5. "Watch the colour after firing."
6. "The gift people keep using."
7. "You asked if it survives a microwave."
8. "Our worst batch this year."
9. "Three minutes of clay, one mistake."
10. "Before you buy handmade plates, check this."

Only use claims you can show.

**Trial Reels**, where available, let you show a Reel to non-followers first. Use them for different ideas, not tiny edits of one video.

## 5. Carousels That Get Saved

```text
Slide 1: Which mug size fits your coffee?
Slide 2: Most cafes serve 180-240 ml. Measure yours.
Slide 3: 180 ml: espresso with milk [photo in hand]
Slide 4: 250 ml: everyday coffee
Slide 5: 350 ml: big tea, soup
Slide 6: How to measure your favourite cup
Slide 7: Save this before you order
```

A save is a promise to return. Make something worth returning to.

## 6. Captions

Structure: **first line** (why read) → **useful detail** → **proof** → **one action**.

❌ **Weak**
```text
New mugs!! Who wants?? Comment YES!!! #love #instagood #viral
```

✅ **Strong**
```text
Tea stains aren't damage. They come off.
1. Mix baking soda and water into a paste.
2. Wait 10 minutes. 3. Wipe gently.
We tested this on 8 glazes; skip it on the gold-rim cups.
Full care guide is in the link.
#stoneware #potterycare #kolkatapottery
```

## 7. Stories and Community

A simple daily Story arc: **context → action → question → result**.

```text
1 Clip: "Opening the kiln in an hour."
2 Poll: "Blue or green came out better?"
3 Reveal
4 Link: "Two green mugs are up."
```

Reply to comments that ask real questions. Turn the best into new posts. Set a schedule: 20 focused minutes a day beats being always half-online.

### DM flow

1. Answer the question first.
2. Ask one useful question.
3. Recommend one option.
4. Send the matching link.
5. Stop if they don't reply after one polite follow-up.

```text
Them: Is this microwave safe?
You: Yes, all pieces without gold rims. Is it for daily use or a gift?
Them: Gift for my sister, she loves tea.
You: The 350 ml green one suits tea. It can reach Pune by Friday if
ordered today before 2 PM: {{link}}
```

Automation can send a requested link after a keyword comment. Make sure the person asked, the message is expected and platform rules allow it.

## 8. Collaborate

Use Collab posts with cafes, home cooks and interior creators whose audience matches. Agree on who posts, who replies, rights and disclosure. A 6,000-follower local food creator may be worth more than a 400,000-follower comedy page.

## 9. Shopping and Links

Product tagging, shops and checkout differ by country and account. Whatever is available, the path must be clear: post → profile or tag → matching page. Use a different UTM for bio, Stories and creators.

## 10. Measure

| Question | Look at |
|---|---|
| Did it reach new people? | reach and non-follower share |
| Did it hold them? | watch time, retention curve |
| Was it useful? | saves and shares per reach |
| Did they get curious? | profile visits, follows |
| Did business move? | link visits, enquiries, sales |

```text
Save rate = saves / reach
Profile visit rate = profile visits / reach
```

Compare posts **with the same job**. Judging a size guide by reach is like judging a cookbook by its cover price.

### A four-week test

| Week | Plan |
|---|---|
| 1 | 4 topics, normal openings |
| 2 | same topics, new openings |
| 3 | best two topics as Reel and carousel |
| 4 | best format, stronger next step |

Then decide: one thing to repeat, one to improve, one to stop.

### 💻 Tech Lens
Export insights weekly or use an approved API into a sheet with post ID, job, pillar, format, opening type, reach, saves, shares, profile visits and link sessions. Compare medians by job, since one viral outlier distorts averages. Don't use scraping tools that ask for your password.

> 👔 **Owner's rule:** if a post can't be linked to one of five jobs (reach, trust, teach, community, convert), don't publish it.

## 11. Safety

- Two-factor login and separate staff access.
- Don't buy followers, likes or engagement pods.
- Ask before reposting customer content.
- Moderate with public rules.
- Keep copies of your content and an email list outside the platform.

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- The profile is a landing page.
- Choose formats by job.
- Saves, shares and replies often tell you more than likes.
- Move willing people to a list you control.
