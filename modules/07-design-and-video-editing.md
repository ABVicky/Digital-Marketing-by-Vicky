# 🎬 Module 07 — Design & Video Editing

> 🔵 Stage 2 · Content & Search · ⏱️ ~5 hours

Two people film the same mug falling onto a table. One video starts with a logo animation, a greeting and six seconds of walking. The other starts with the mug already in the air. Same phone, same mug, same studio. Editing is mostly deciding what people *don't* have to sit through.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Apply six design rules to posts, ads and thumbnails.
- Build a small brand kit and reusable templates in Canva.
- Shoot and edit a short vertical video in VN or a similar editor.
- Export, name and store assets without licence trouble.

---

## 1. Choose Tools by Job

| Job | Beginner-friendly tools | Step up |
|---|---|---|
| Posts, carousels, simple decks | Canva | Figma, Adobe Illustrator |
| Photo edits | phone editor, Canva, Snapseed | Photoshop, Lightroom |
| Short video on phone | VN, InShot, Edits | DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro |
| Screen recording | built-in recorder, OBS | same, with better audio |
| Captions | editor auto-captions, then manual correction | dedicated caption tools |
| Audio clean-up | editor noise reduction | Audacity, DaVinci Fairlight |

Learn one design tool and one editor well. Switching tools every month is a popular way to avoid making anything.

> ⚠️ Features and prices change, and some apps aren't available everywhere. CapCut, for example, is reported as banned or not officially available in India. Choose a tool you can install officially, and avoid modified app files.

## 2. Six Design Rules

1. **Contrast:** make the important thing clearly different.
2. **Alignment:** line things up on purpose.
3. **Repetition:** repeat fonts, colours and spacing.
4. **Proximity:** keep related things together.
5. **Hierarchy:** decide what is seen first, second and third.
6. **Restraint:** remove until it breaks, then put one thing back.

❌ **Weak post**
```text
Logo big at the top. Seven fonts. Four stickers. Five offers.
Phone, email, address, website and two QR codes.
"Contact us for more info."
```

✅ **Strong post**
```text
Photo: two hands shaping a bowl, filling most of the frame.
Headline (largest): Make your first bowl on Saturday
Small line: 2 hours. Clay and firing included. ₹1,500
Bottom: 3 seats left (only if true) | Link in bio
Small logo in one corner.
```

**The squint test:** shrink the design to a thumbnail and squint. Can you still see the main message? Most people will meet it that small.

## 3. Build a Brand Kit

| Item | Mitti example |
|---|---|
| Main colour | clay brown |
| Accent | deep green |
| Neutral | warm off-white |
| Heading font | one readable serif |
| Body font | one clean sans-serif |
| Photo style | daylight, hands visible, no heavy filter |
| Voice | warm, plain, specific |
| Never | fake steam, stock families, countdown stickers |

Store exact colour codes and font names. Canva's plans differ in how many brand kits and resizing features they include, so check the [pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/). On a free plan you can still save a template with your colours and make each size manually.

## 4. Canva: A Repeatable Workflow

1. Create the correct size first.
2. Make five master templates: quote, tip carousel, offer, proof, event.
3. Lock logo and margins where the plan allows.
4. Duplicate the master; never edit it directly.
5. Replace text and image.
6. Check spelling, price, date and contrast.
7. Export and name the file.

### Common sizes

| Use | Ratio | Typical working size |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical video, Stories, Shorts | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 |
| Portrait feed post | 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 |
| Square | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 |
| Landscape video, YouTube | 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 |
| YouTube thumbnail | 16:9 | 1280 × 720 |

Platforms crop previews differently. Keep text away from the edges, and check the live preview.

### Carousel structure

```text
1  Promise or tension
2  Why it matters
3-6 One step each, one visual each
7  Summary worth saving
8  One natural next step
```

Every slide should make sense if someone shares only that slide.

## 5. Shoot So Editing Is Easy

| Element | Simple rule |
|---|---|
| Light | face a window; turn off the mixed yellow light |
| Sound | get close, quiet room, record a test |
| Steadiness | tripod, or elbows on a table |
| Lens | wipe it |
| Framing | fill the frame with what matters |
| Variety | wide, medium, close-up of the same action |
| Space | hold each shot two seconds before and after |

People forgive a plain picture sooner than bad sound.

### Shot list for "Can our parcel survive this drop?"

```text
1  Close: box leaving hands (the opening)
2  Wide: the fall and impact
3  Close: opening the box
4  Close: checking each mug
5  Medium: Meera's reaction
6  Close: packing layers
7  Wide: shelf, one line of text with the next step
```

## 6. Edit in VN (or Any Timeline Editor)

1. **Set the canvas** to 9:16.
2. **Import** and lay clips in story order.
3. **Rough cut:** remove every breath, repeat and "umm" that adds nothing.
4. **Move the best moment to the front.**
5. **Audio:** level the voice, then add quiet music you may use.
6. **Captions:** auto-generate, then fix names, numbers and Hindi/Bengali/English mix.
7. **On-screen text:** one short line at a time.
8. **Colour:** small exposure and white-balance corrections.
9. **Sound check** on phone speaker and earphones.
10. **Export** at the source resolution, usually 1080p is enough.

VN's app-store listing describes free, watermark-free export up to 4K and 60 fps, with optional paid upgrades. Check your own store listing, because regions and plans vary.

### Five edits that solve most problems

| Problem | Edit |
|---|---|
| People leave early | start at the action |
| Feels slow | cut pauses, change angle |
| Hard to follow | add one line of text per step |
| Feels like an ad | show the test before the claim |
| Weak ending | stop right after the payoff |

> 🔑 Transitions rarely fix a dull story. A cut on movement is usually invisible and better.

## 7. Thumbnails and Covers

A thumbnail must answer "what will I get?" in under a second.

- One subject, large.
- Three to five words at most, adding to the title instead of repeating it.
- Strong contrast between subject and background.
- Honest: the video must deliver what it shows.

Make three versions. View them at the size of a fingernail beside competing results.

## 8. Rights and Consent

| Asset | Before you use it |
|---|---|
| Music | check licence for **business** use on that platform |
| Stock photo/video | keep the licence record |
| Font | check commercial and web use |
| Customer video | written permission, uses and duration |
| Children | guardian consent and extra care |
| AI image/voice | permission for likeness, disclosure rules, accuracy |
| Trending clip | don't assume reuse is allowed |

Many trending songs are licensed for personal accounts but not for business promotion. If unsure, use a licensed library or original sound.

```text
  01_brief/
  02_raw/
  03_audio/
  04_project/
  05_exports/
  06_licences/
```

```text

date_campaign_concept_version_ratio_language
```

Keep raw footage for strong ideas. Next month you may need a 16:9 version.

> 👔 **Batching:** film one afternoon a month, with four ideas and three hooks each. You get twelve possible openings from one set-up.

> 💻 **Automation:** use `ffmpeg` for repeatable jobs such as compressing a folder or making a 9:16 canvas.

### 💻 Useful ffmpeg Commands

```bash
ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf "scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=1080:1920:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2" -c:v libx264 -crf 22 -c:a aac output_9x16.mp4
```

This fits the whole frame inside 1080 × 1920 and adds bars where needed. Keep the original; write to a new file.

## 10. Quality Check

- [ ] The first second shows the tension or result
- [ ] Understandable without sound
- [ ] Voice is clear with sound
- [ ] Names, price and date are right
- [ ] Text stays inside safe areas
- [ ] Claims are true and disclosed where needed
- [ ] Music, fonts and people are cleared
- [ ] Alternative text or captions added
- [ ] File named and archived

## 🏋️ Exercises

### Exercise 7.1: Fix the opening
A 40-second video starts: logo (3 s), "Hello friends" (4 s), walk to the wheel (6 s), then the bowl collapses beautifully. Re-edit it on paper.

Show three thumbnails to five people for one second each. Record what they think the video is about.

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- One message per design.
- Good sound and light beat expensive effects.
- Put the best moment first.
- Keep proof of rights with the project.

## ✅ Quick Quiz

1. Your video loses people in two seconds. What first?
   - A) Add a longer logo intro
   - B) Open with the result or tension
   - C) Add transitions
2. A trending song is available in the app. Can a business always use it?
   - A) Yes
   - B) No, check business rights
   - C) Only at night
3. Which ratio fits a vertical short video?
   - A) 16:9
   - B) 1:1
   - C) 9:16

<details>
<summary>View Answers</summary>

1. **B.** Earn attention before explaining.
2. **B.** Personal availability is not a commercial licence.
3. **C.** Vertical video fills the phone.

</details>
