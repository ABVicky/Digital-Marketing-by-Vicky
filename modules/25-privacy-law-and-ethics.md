# 🛡️ Module 25 — Privacy, Law & Ethics

> 🟣 Stage 4 · Measure & Scale · ⏱️ ~4 hours

If a shopkeeper followed you home, guessed your worries and wrote them on your door, you wouldn't call it personalization. Digital tools make similar behaviour easy and invisible.

> ⚠️ This module is education, not legal advice. Laws depend on place, industry and facts. Review official sources and use a qualified adviser.

> 🔑 **In short:** Ethical digital marketing collects only needed data, asks for specific permission, tells the truth, makes leaving easy and prepares for mistakes. In India, check DPDP phases, consumer-protection and ASCI rules, and get legal advice for real decisions.

## 1. Data Map

| Data | Purpose | Source | Access | Keep until |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| order address | delivery/accounts | checkout | operations | legal and business need |
| email permission | chosen emails | form | marketing | withdrawn / no longer needed |
| analytics event | improve site | consented tag | analyst | set period |

If you can't explain the purpose, don't collect it.

### Map the life of one email address

```text
Collect: gift guide form, stated purpose
Use: send guide, then emails selected by person
Share: approved email provider, contract checked
Protect: role access and two-factor login
Keep: while needed/permitted; review inactive records
Change: preferences honoured
Delete/restrict: request and lawful duty process
```

Now test it. Can you find where the address is stored, who can export it, how it's corrected, and whether an opt-out reaches WhatsApp as well as email if it should? A policy that nobody can perform isn't protection.

### Separate purposes

| Data | Purpose A | Purpose B | Don't assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery phone | courier | workshop offers | A permits B |
| Class allergy note | safety | audience targeting | A permits B |
| Analytics event | fix checkout | identify a named visitor | A permits B |
| Review | product learning | paid ad testimonial | A permits B |

## 2. Consent

Use specific choices, plain notice, no pre-ticked box and withdrawal as easy as giving permission. Keep evidence. Don't assume one consent covers every purpose or channel.

India's DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 with phased application. Consent-manager provisions were scheduled for November 2026, and most core duties for **May 2027** after 18 months. Sources differ by about a day; check the [official rules](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf) and any later amendment.

Other places may apply GDPR/UK GDPR, ePrivacy, state privacy, telemarketing or anti-spam laws, sometimes because of where recipients live. Indian commercial SMS and calls also have telecom rules. Get the facts before sending.

### A form makeover

❌ **Weak**
```text
Phone *  Email *  Date of birth *
[x] I agree to everything.
Submit
```

✅ **Strong**
```text
Email for your size guide *
[ ] Email me useful pottery offers about twice a month.
Privacy notice: what we collect, why, how long, sharing and choices.
[Send the guide]
```

The guide can be sent without marketing consent. The optional box isn't pre-ticked, and the privacy notice should be understandable at the point of collection. Your notice and legal basis need professional review for your facts.

### Withdrawal and rights

Create one internal request process: verify identity proportionately, find relevant data, pause affected uses, respond within the applicable time, record the decision and inform processors. Don't demand excessive identity documents for a simple unsubscribe.

### DPDP transition check

India's official rules were notified with phases extending to 2027. Today, document readiness gaps and confirm effective dates, then make a dated plan for notices, choices, rights, security, vendors, retention, children and incidents. Later amendments or guidance may change it.

## 3. Cookies and Tags

A banner isn't protection if tags fire before a choice. Test first visit, accept, reject, change and withdrawal. Keep "reject" understandable. Server-side tracking doesn't remove consent duties.

### 💻 Test Matrix

| State | Necessary | Analytics | Ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| no choice | site only | follow applicable law/configuration | follow applicable law/configuration |
| reject | yes | no optional tag | no optional tag |
| accept analytics | yes | yes | no |
| withdraw | yes | stop future optional use | stop future optional use |

Consent signals also need correct vendor configuration. Don't assume a plugin did it.

## 4. Claims and Reviews

❌ **Risky**
```text
India's #1 mug. Guaranteed never to break.
```

✅ **Supportable**
```text
Broken on arrival? Send a photo within 48 hours and we replace it.
```

Keep dated evidence for important claims. Comparisons must be fair. Reviews need a genuine source; testimonials mustn't hide that the result is unusual. Health, finance, employment, education and children need extra care.

### Test claims before ads

| Claim | Evidence needed | Safer alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Best in India" | fair, current comparison | explain measured difference |
| "Saves 30%" | same basket, dates, method | list price difference |
| "Guaranteed marks" | exceptional and likely risky | describe syllabus and support |
| "No side effects" | qualified proof/review | avoid unsupported health claim |
| "Eco-friendly" | defined, full scope | state specific certified material/process |
| "Only 2 left" | live stock | show accurate stock or remove |

❌ **Weak testimonial**
```text
Earned ₹5 lakh in a month! (paid actor)
```

✅ **Stronger testimonial**
```text
"The worksheet showed me two product costs I'd forgotten."
— {{permissioned customer}}, {{date}}. Individual experience.
```

A disclosure can't make a false or unfair claim true.

## 5. Influencers and AI

Make material connections obvious at the start or where people see the endorsement. Don't hide disclosure after "more". Agree on claim approval and correction. Check ASCI and consumer-law rules in India and local rules elsewhere. Obtain permission for likeness and voice.

## 6. Dark Patterns

| Pattern | Better |
|---|---|
| fake scarcity | true stock or no counter |
| hidden fees | full price early |
| confirm shaming | neutral choices |
| forced action | let people buy without irrelevant permission |
| subscription trap | easy cancellation |
| bait and switch | advertise what is available |
| disguised ad | clear label |

India has specific consumer-protection guidance on dark patterns. Review the [Department of Consumer Affairs](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/).

## 7. Responsible Targeting

Don't infer or exploit sensitive conditions. Avoid excluding people from important opportunities. Review outputs by group where lawful and appropriate. Give vulnerable people more protection, not more pressure.

## 8. Incident Plan

1. Stop the harmful send/tag/access.
2. Preserve logs without spreading personal data.
3. Escalate to the named privacy/security owner.
4. Assess who and what is affected.
5. Meet required notice duties.
6. Correct, document and prevent recurrence.

Prepare this before you need it. Don't quietly delete evidence.

> 👔 **Newspaper test:** would the customer think this practice was fair if you explained it plainly on the front page?

## 9. Platform Rules and Regulated Work

Platform approval doesn't prove legality, and legality doesn't prove a platform will approve. Keep current official policies for:

- Health and medicines.
- Finance, loans and investments.
- Employment, housing and credit.
- Education outcomes and minors.
- Alcohol, gambling and other restricted products.
- Political and social issues.
- Copyright, trademark and endorsements.
- Commercial messages, calls and email.

Have a qualified person review high-risk claims. Avoid private medical or financial attributes in audience choices. Don't upload customer data before confirming consent/legal basis, purpose, platform terms and deletion.

## 10. Consent and Tracking Test Script

| Step | Expected |
|---|---|
| First visit | no non-essential processing before permitted choice |
| Reject | site works; optional tags off |
| Accept analytics | analytics only as chosen |
| Change choice | new setting takes effect |
| Withdraw | future processing stops; downstream process starts |
| Different device | preference limitation understood |
| Email opt-out | promotional email suppressed |
| Delete/correct request | recorded, routed, completed |

Use a browser network inspector and your vendor's test tools. A banner screenshot alone isn't evidence.

### 💻 URL Privacy Check

```python
from urllib.parse import urlparse, parse_qs

private_keys = {"email", "phone", "name", "token"}

def risky_query_keys(url):
    keys = set(parse_qs(urlparse(url).query))
    return sorted(keys & private_keys)

print(risky_query_keys("https://example.com/thanks?utm_source=email&email=x@example.com"))
```

This catches only known names. Review forms, hashes, event payloads, logs, referrers and custom parameters too. Never use real customer data in this test.

## 11. Marketing to Children and Vulnerable People

Use age-appropriate language, stronger defaults, parental involvement where required, no exploitative pressure and limited data. Don't target children for tracking or collect extra data because it improves a segment. Stop using fear, shame or medical vulnerability to increase conversion.

### Accessibility and dignity

Don't present disability as tragedy to sell a product. Ask people how they want to be described, pay contributors fairly and test the actual task. Inclusive images don't replace accessible checkout.

## 12. AI and Synthetic Advertising

| Use | Check |
|---|---|
| Generated background | doesn't misrepresent product/context |
| Synthetic voice | identity, permission, disclosure |
| AI reviewer | never present as real reviewer |
| Translated celebrity | rights and misleading effect |
| Chatbot recommendation | disclosure, accuracy, handoff |

ASCI's final synthetic-content guidelines are reported to begin 1 January 2027 and use risk tiers. Review the [official ASCI material](https://www.ascionline.in/) before publication. Even low-risk AI content can make a misleading claim.

## 13. Dark-Pattern Audit

| Page element | Check |
|---|---|
| Price | all mandatory costs shown early |
| Choice | "no" as clear as "yes" |
| Timer | actual deadline and time zone |
| Stock | live, updated quantity |
| Consent | separate purposes, unticked |
| Subscription | renewal, price and cancellation clear |
| Review | genuine, not selected deceptively |
| Delivery | realistic range and exceptions |

```text
Before: "Keep my savings" vs "No, I'd rather overpay"
After:  "Keep discount" vs "Continue without discount"
```

Choose neutral labels. Test whether people can cancel as easily as they joined.

## 14. Incident Tabletop

Scenario: a staff member emails 300 workshop customers with everyone's addresses visible.

| Time | Practice action |
|---|---|
| 0 min | stop further sends, preserve message and recipient list |
| 5 min | tell named privacy/security owner |
| 20 min | assess data, number, recipients and continuing exposure |
| 45 min | get required legal/privacy advice, contain and notify as required |
| Later | correct process, train, document decision |

Don't promise a universal notification time here: laws and facts differ. The lesson is to have the contact, evidence and decision process ready.

> 🔑 The safest campaign is often the one with less data, a clearer choice and a promise the business can keep.

## 15. Ethics Scorecard

Score 0–2: truthful, necessary, expected, reversible, accessible, fair, secure, accountable. Anything scoring zero needs a named person, a fix and a review. A campaign with a high conversion rate doesn't average away a privacy failure.

## 🛠️ Practical Lab: Make a Small Business Privacy- and Claim-Ready

**You will finish with:** a data map, tested consent, a claims file and an incident card. Time: 4 hours. This is education, not legal advice.

### Tools and official places

| Job | Tool | Open |
|---|---|---|
| DPDP rules | official PDF | [PIB document](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf) |
| Advertising code | ASCI | [ascionline.in](https://www.ascionline.in/) |
| Consumer guidance | Department of Consumer Affairs | [consumeraffairs.nic.in](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/) |
| Commercial SMS/calls (India) | TRAI | [trai.gov.in](https://www.trai.gov.in/) |
| Trademark search (India) | IP India | [ipindia.gov.in](https://ipindia.gov.in/) |
| Consent tool | a certified consent platform or your site platform's tool | compare first |
| Check tags | browser Developer Tools, Tag Assistant | Module 21 |
| Accessibility | WAVE | [wave.webaim.org](https://wave.webaim.org/) |

### Step 1: data map (45 minutes)

1. List every form, app, sheet and inbox holding personal data.
2. Fill: `data, purpose, source, notice/consent, tool, who can see, keep until, how to delete`.
3. Highlight anything with no purpose. Plan its removal.
4. Export lists nobody needs? Delete safely after checking legal duties.

### Step 2: fix one form (30 minutes)

Remove one unnecessary field. Write purpose and frequency, separate optional consent (unticked) and link the notice. Submit as a tester, then withdraw.

### Step 3: test your tags (45 minutes)

1. Open a private window → Developer Tools → **Network** → filter `collect`, `facebook`, `clarity`.
2. Load the page **before** choosing. Note what fires.
3. Reject → reload → note. Accept → note. Change choice → note.
4. Compare with what your notice says. Log defects for the developer.

### Step 4: claims file (45 minutes)

```text
Claim ID | exact words | where used | evidence file/date | approved by | review date
C01 | 0 of 10 boxes broke in our drop test | gift page | test_2026-10.xlsx | Meera | 2027-04
```

Check your last ten posts and ads. Remove or rewrite each claim without evidence.

### Step 5: influencer and AI disclosure (20 minutes)

1. List gifts, payments, employees and relatives who post.
2. Agree on clear disclosure wording and placement.
3. List AI-made images/voices/text used in ads; decide disclosure using current ASCI and platform rules.

### Step 6: incident card (20 minutes)

```text
Stop: who can pause sends/tags/ads?
Tell: privacy owner {{name/phone}} and backup
Keep: screenshots, time, list of systems (no forwarding of personal data)
Ask: who and what is affected?
Advice: lawyer/adviser contact
Notify: as law requires
Repair and review date:
```

Run a 15-minute practice: *"An email showed 300 addresses."*

### Commercial SMS in India

Business SMS normally goes through telecom operators' **DLT** registration (entity, header, template and consent rules). Register through your operator or provider, use approved templates and check TRAI's current rules.

### If it goes wrong

| Problem | Do |
|---|---|
| Tags fire before choice | pause them, fix consent order, retest |
| Can't find a person's data | improve map; search by system |
| Vendor won't explain data use | don't send data |
| Bought list found | stop using it, get advice, document |

## ❓ Common Questions

### What is DPDP and how does it affect marketing?

DPDP is India's personal-data protection law. The 2025 rules apply in phases, with most core duties scheduled for May 2027. Marketers should prepare clear notices, specific consent, easy withdrawal, retention limits and vendor controls.

### What are dark patterns in marketing?

They are designs that trick or pressure people, such as fake scarcity, hidden fees, pre-ticked consent, shaming choices and difficult cancellation. Show true stock and full cost, and make "no" as easy as "yes".

### Do influencers have to disclose paid promotions?

They should clearly disclose material connections such as payment, gifted products or family and work links. Follow current ASCI and consumer-law guidance in India, or local rules where the audience lives.
