# 📍 Module 10 — Local SEO & AI Search

> 🔵 Stage 2 · Content & Search · ⏱️ ~3 hours

Someone stands outside a metro station and searches "pottery class open today". They need distance, timing and confidence. A 3,000-word essay won't help. A correct profile might.

> 🔑 **In short:** SEO helps pages rank in search, AEO helps content answer a question directly, and GEO helps it be used and cited in AI-generated answers. They share one foundation: accessible pages, clear answers, original evidence and a trustworthy source.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Improve a local business presence.

## 1. Local Visibility

Google describes local ranking in terms of **relevance, distance and prominence**. You can clarify relevance and build prominence; you cannot move every searcher closer.

### Profile checklist

- [ ] Real business name, no added keywords
- [ ] Accurate primary category
- [ ] Correct pin, phone, website and hours
- [ ] Services and accessibility details
- [ ] Recent, genuine photos
- [ ] Holiday hours
- [ ] Questions and reviews handled

Keep the name, address and phone consistent on your website and major directories. If you visit customers, follow service-area rules and don't publish a fake office.

### Verification and common problems

| Problem | First step |
|---|---|
| Can't verify | follow the offered method; keep business documents consistent |
| Duplicate profile | report/merge through official process |
| Suspended | read guidelines, correct facts, appeal with evidence |
| Wrong pin | edit, then test directions on two phones |
| Someone suggested wrong hours | review alerts weekly |

Don't create a second profile because the first is suspended. It usually makes recovery harder.

### A description that helps

❌ **Weak**
```text
Best pottery class Kolkata cheap pottery class near me best pottery.
```

✅ **Strong**
```text
Small pottery classes for beginners, couples and children in Kolkata.
Wheel and hand-building sessions include clay and firing. Booking ahead
is recommended. No steps at the entrance; parking on the next lane.
```

Choose only true details. Accessibility information helps people decide whether they can come.

## 2. Local Pages That Deserve to Exist

A good location page includes real directions, staff, photos, available services, local reviews and parking. Copying one page for 50 neighbourhoods and changing only the place name is thin doorway content.

## 3. Reviews

❌ **Weak**
```text
Give us 5 stars and get 10% off.
```

✅ **Strong**
```text
Thank you for joining Saturday's class. If you have a minute, please
share an honest review here: {{link}}. Your feedback, good or bad,
helps other beginners decide.
```

Ask all relevant customers, not only happy ones. Don't buy, gate or fake reviews.

### Reply to a complaint

1. Thank and acknowledge.
2. Avoid revealing private details.
3. State the next step.
4. Move personal resolution to a private channel.
5. Fix the cause.

```text
Thanks for telling us, and I'm sorry the class started 25 minutes late.
Please contact {{support}} so we can look into your booking. We have
changed the gap between sessions to prevent another delay.
```

Only mention a fix if it actually happened.

### Build a steady review habit

1. Decide the moment: after pickup, not before the result is known.
2. Ask in person, then send one link.
3. Ask every customer in that process.
4. Reply in your own words.
5. Tag themes: teaching, parking, delay, value.
6. Change one cause each month.

Review velocity isn't a game. A sudden burst of similar reviews looks strange to people and may be filtered.

### When a review is false

Check records calmly. Reply with limited facts, report if it breaks policy, and move on. Never publish a customer's private details to "prove" a point.

```text
We can't find a booking matching this visit, but we'd like to understand.
Please contact {{support}} with the date, without posting personal
details here.
```

## 4. AI Search: What Changes?

AI Overviews, AI Mode and assistants can summarize several sources and answer follow-up questions. Some searches may result in fewer site visits. Your goal becomes broader: be understandable, trustworthy and worth visiting.

Google says appearing as a supporting link in its AI features needs no special technical requirement beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet, and recommends normal SEO foundations. Read [its guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

| Useful work | Unsupported shortcut |
|---|---|
| Clear answers and original evidence | secret "AI keyword density" |
| Crawlable text and accurate facts | fake author expertise |
| Consistent business information | mass AI pages |
| Comparisons and limitations | promises of guaranteed citations |

"AEO" and "GEO" are names for optimizing for answers and generative engines. Treat confident formulas with caution. Systems and results change by user, time and query.

### SEO, AEO and GEO compared

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full name | search engine optimization | answer engine optimization | generative engine optimization |
| Aims to be | found in results | the direct answer | used or cited in an AI answer |
| Main work | intent, pages, links, crawling | short accurate answers, clear headings | original evidence, consistent facts, independent mentions |
| Useful signs | impressions, clicks, enquiries | answer features, calls, saved time | correct mentions, cited pages, referrals |
| Can it be guaranteed? | no | no | no |

Google's [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) treats AEO and GEO as part of SEO. It says Google Search doesn't need `llms.txt`, special AI schema or content cut into small pieces, and it warns against mass pages made for every query variation. Its documentation also lists FAQ rich results as **ended on 7 May 2026**.

Other assistants work differently. A [Vercel and MERJ analysis](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler) found that major AI crawlers it studied didn't run JavaScript, so put important text in the HTML itself.

### A page that serves all three

1. Ask one real question in the heading.
2. Answer it accurately in two or three sentences.
3. Add evidence only you have: a test, photo, number or case.
4. State limits and the date you checked.
5. Name a responsible author.
6. Link to related decisions.
7. Keep it crawlable without scripts.

## 5. Make an Answer Useful

```text
Question: Is Mitti's stoneware dishwasher safe?
Short answer: Yes, for pieces marked dishwasher safe.
Evidence: Our test method, number of cycles and date.
Limits: Hand-wash metallic details.
Next step: Care guide and product list.
```

This works for people, search and assistants. Never invent the test.

## 6. Measure

| Signal | Source | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Calls, directions | business profile | not every action becomes a sale |
| Search clicks | Search Console | AI-feature traffic may be combined with web totals |
| Referrals | analytics | assistants don't always pass a source |
| Mentions | sampled prompts | answers vary |
| "How did you hear?" | form or sales team | memory is imperfect |

> 👔 **Monthly local routine:** check facts, add photos, answer reviews, fix one complaint pattern and count qualified calls.

> 💻 **Crawler controls:** review official user-agent documentation before blocking. Blocking a training crawler, search crawler and user-triggered fetcher can have different consequences.

## 7. Local Content That Isn't Filler

| Idea | Why it earns attention |
|---|---|
| "Parking and metro guide to our lane" | removes real friction |
| Monsoon class policy | answers repeated calls |
| Children's workshop by age | helps parents choose |
| Partner cafes using our cups | proof and links |
| Local festival making day | event and community |

One excellent location page beats 40 copies. If you serve several areas from one studio, explain travel and delivery honestly instead of inventing branches.

## 8. Offline Meets Online

- Put the booking QR code where people wait; include a short typed link too.
- Use separate links for cafe cards, packaging and school leaflets.
- Train staff to ask "How did you find us?" without pressure.
- Check that phone calls are answered during advertised hours.
- Update hours before every festival.

```text
Missed-call rate = missed calls / all calls
```

If 30 of 100 calls are missed, improving answer coverage may be cheaper than buying 30 more calls.

## 9. Local Ads Alongside Organic

Search ads can fill gaps while local visibility grows. Keep radius realistic: would someone really cross the city for a ₹1,500 class? Exclude areas you can't serve, schedule around answered hours, and compare **booked seats**, not direction clicks.

## 10. A Practical AI-Search Plan

| Week | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | list 20 real questions |
| 2 | check facts across website, profile and directories |
| 3 | improve five answers with evidence |
| 4 | add comparison and limitations |
| 5 | earn one independent mention |
| 6 | sample questions again |

### Sampling without fooling yourself

Ask the same question on several days and in a clean session. Record exact wording, date, tool, whether web sources were used, your mention, correctness and cited pages.

| Question | Run | Mentioned | Correct | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| beginner pottery Kolkata | 1 | yes | wrong price | old directory |
| beginner pottery Kolkata | 2 | no | – | – |
| beginner pottery Kolkata | 3 | yes | yes | own page |

"Mentioned 2 of 3 times" is a small sample, not a ranking. The useful finding is the **old directory with a wrong price**: fix it.

## 11. What to Do When Clicks Fall

1. Separate brand, informational and commercial queries.
2. Check impressions, position and result layout.
3. Find pages whose whole value was a short fact.
4. Add what a summary can't easily replace: tools, original images, comparison, booking, community.
5. Build direct relationships: email, repeat visits, offline.
6. Compare enquiries and revenue, not only clicks.

❌ **Weak reaction**
```text
Publish 500 AI answers before competitors do.
```

✅ **Strong reaction**
```text
Turn "what size mug" into an interactive size chooser with real photos,
keep the direct answer, and ask whether readers want the care guide.
```

Google has been testing more owner controls and reporting for AI features; availability varies by country and account. Check Search Console and its documentation rather than assuming you have them.

### 💻 Consistency Check

```python
facts = {
    "website": {"phone": "+91-33-0000-0000", "price": 1500},
    "directory": {"phone": "+91-33-0000-0000", "price": 1200},
}

baseline = facts["website"]
for source, values in facts.items():
    for key, value in baseline.items():
        if values.get(key) != value:
            print(f"{source}: {key} is {values.get(key)!r}, expected {value!r}")
```

Maintain a small approved fact sheet: name, address, hours, price, policies and last review. The phone number above is a placeholder.

## ❓ Common Questions

### What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) aims for visibility in search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) aims to be the direct answer. GEO (generative engine optimization) aims to be used or cited in AI answers. Google treats the last two as part of SEO.

### Does llms.txt help with AI search?

Google says its Search AI features don't use it. A 2026 Ahrefs study reported that 97% of llms.txt files it examined received no traffic in May 2026. It is harmless, but clear crawlable pages and original evidence deserve the effort.

### How do I rank in Google Maps?

Google describes local results using relevance, distance and prominence. Keep a verified profile accurate, choose the right category, earn honest reviews and publish useful local pages. You can't control a searcher's distance.
